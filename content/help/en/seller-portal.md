Understand the seller view and which features the store needs to enable.

## The seller's own view
Sellers sign in with the confirmed email linked to their seller profile. The portal shows their own details and items in the selected store, not the staff workspace.

Sellers can read and accept published agreements and enter bank details. A separate review link can let them approve or decline a proposal after signing in with the correct account.

## Features the store chooses
- Self-registration must be enabled before the store's registration link can be used. Staff review the applications.
- Announced drop-offs require the relevant store setting. An announcement does not become a received drop-off until the store receives it.
- Photo submissions depend on the service feature `KOMISIO_SELLER_SUBMISSIONS_ENABLED` being enabled.

> A seller's response to an item proposal does not automatically receive the item, publish it to the POS or approve a payout.
