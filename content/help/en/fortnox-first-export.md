Work with one day at a time and check the company, date and amounts before sending\.

1. Open Day closes and exports and generate the close for the correct date\. This records a summary in Komisio, not a Fortnox voucher\.
2. Review the preview: accounts, debit, credit and unmapped amounts\. Review the mapping with your accountant\. Balanced debit and credit do not by themselves establish correct accounting\.
3. Choose Create accounting documents to save the accounting basis\. You can then download the file or, as owner/administrator, use Send to Fortnox\. Avoid both importing the file and sending the same export directly\.
4. A real send creates a Fortnox voucher\. Its reference appears once confirmed\. If the outcome is uncertain, open the send recovery help and do not send again\.

> Automatic sending is a separate owner choice for prepared exports\. Day closes remain manual\. Connecting or reading help does not enable automation\.
