Find and edit items, select several at once and follow up prices and selling periods.

## Work in the item table
1. Search or filter the items. Open an item for pictures, history and further details.
2. Click the item name to open the item. The pencil beside it edits the name. You can also edit category, item type and assortment group when those columns are shown.
3. Select one or more rows for the available shared actions, such as editing several items or printing A4 label sheets.

The default view shows item, price, seller and status. Open **Columns** to show more details. Your choice is saved for this store in your browser and also applies to the handover item list.

Categories, item types, attributes and assortment groups are managed in the store's Item settings. Changed display text does not replace older agreement or sale evidence.

On the item page, **Change price** sits beside the current price for items still on sale. You need permission to edit items.

## Follow up the selling period
Open Selling periods to see upcoming or pending actions. Prices and dates follow the item's saved terms. Check the item and proposed action before making a change.

> A pending markdown symbol asks you to follow up the item. Check its current price and history to see what has actually happened.
