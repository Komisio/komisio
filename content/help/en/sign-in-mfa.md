Manage your account and get help if you cannot sign in.

## Your account
In My account you can change profile details, language and password. Use an email address you can access and complete confirmation when requested.

## Add extra sign-in protection
1. Open two-factor authentication on the account page.
2. Add the account to your authenticator app by scanning the QR code privately.
3. Enter the app's current code to confirm activation.

When two-factor authentication is required, enter a current code during sign-in.

## If you are locked out
Use password recovery on the sign-in page if you have forgotten your password. If you have lost access to your authenticator, contact support for recovery help. Help contains contact details; if you cannot sign in, use the service's support contact.

> Never send passwords, QR codes or current one-time codes to support. Two-factor recovery requires operator checks and is not a shortcut around sign-in protection.
