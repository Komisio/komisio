Review the seller balance, prepare a payout and record it after the money has been paid.

## Prepare the payment
1. Open Payouts and check the seller's available balance. Reserved amounts and the store's minimum payout affect what can be paid.
2. Review a request or select sellers for settlement. Approval reserves the amount.
3. Use the payment sheet to review and make the transfers.

## Bank details and confirmation
Sellers enter bank details in the seller portal. Store owners and administrators can read them on the seller profile and during payout review. Changed bank details may require another review of the payment sheet.

After paying through your bank, record the payment and its reference. You can print the payment sheet or download it as CSV. Seller statements show the recorded amounts.

> Komisio does not transfer the money for you. Mark a payout as paid only after the transfer has actually been made.
