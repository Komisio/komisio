Start with the system you want to connect to Komisio\. Open its section for status and settings\.

1. Choose point of sale, online store or accounting according to what you want to connect\.
2. Read the connection status and follow the steps in that section\. Some actions require an owner or administrator\.
3. Check which information is transferred and in which direction before enabling an automatic feature\.
4. Follow the status of products, receipts, orders or accounting documents within each integration\. Open the details if anything needs attention\.

> A connection does not mean every automatic feature is enabled\. Check them individually in the integration settings\.
