A receiving label keeps goods together during processing\. An item label identifies a registered item and shows its price\.

1. Open the existing drop\-off or item and check the store and reference\. A receiving label does not mean goods have been inspected or accepted for sale\.
2. For an item label, find the registered item, open its label and check description, price and currency\. If the label is unavailable, return to the item and check its details\.
3. Use the page’s print options\. Choose the correct printer and copy count for a connected printer, or review the browser print dialog\. A queued job or open dialog does not prove printing: check the physical label before attaching it\.
4. If a queueing response is missing, use retry in the same open form\. Do not repeatedly create new jobs\. Check the job under Printers and print jobs and ask the printer operator for help when needed\. Use label lookup to open an existing reference\.

> Opening help prints nothing and registers no new item\. Do not duplicate an item to replace its label\. Physical printing and scanning need checking with the store’s equipment\.
