Check recorded sales and prepare the store’s accounting evidence.

## Check the sale
Open Sales and use search and filters to find the event. Open a sale to see its recorded items and amounts.

If a POS sale is missing, check the integration's synchronization status. Do not register the same sale again to resolve an uncertain synchronization.

## Prepare the accounting evidence
1. Open Accounting and choose the date for the day close.
2. Review the amounts and the store's account map before preparing an export.
3. Follow the export in Reconciliation. Use the separate connection and sending guides for the accounting system.

> A day close is saved evidence. It does not mean that the amounts have been booked in your accounting system. Later events may require a new version.
