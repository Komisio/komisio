Invite colleagues and give them the access they need in the correct store.

## Invite a colleague
An owner or administrator can manage team members within their permissions. First check the selected store. Enter the colleague's email address, select a role and check the invitation language before sending.

## Choose a role
- Owners can manage store ownership permissions.
- Administrators can manage many store settings and staff, but do not have every owner permission.
- Staff work with the store's daily workflows.
- Read-only members can view information without changing store data.

Review membership when someone changes responsibilities or leaves. Access applies to the selected store; an invitation to one store does not grant access to other stores.

> Do not share one login. Each colleague should use their own account. Printer devices and automations have separate permissions.
