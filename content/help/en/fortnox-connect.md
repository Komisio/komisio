Connect the store’s company and prepare the account mapping\. Connecting sends no accounting data\.

1. You must be an owner or administrator in Komisio and able to authorize access to the correct Fortnox company\. Check applicable integration and program licences using Fortnox guidance\.
2. Open Accounts and integrations\. Enter the company name as shown in Fortnox and connect\. Sign in to Fortnox, check the company and approve the requested permissions\.
3. On return, check the company name\. Use Check connection for a current check\. Choose the same company when reconnecting\.
4. Save the accounts and debit/credit choices agreed with your accountant\. Komisio does not choose accounts for you\.

> Connected does not mean an export has been sent\. Komisio requests company information and bookkeeping access; the permission includes voucher creation when sending is used\.
